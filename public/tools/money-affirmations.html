Welcome

What is a Money Affirmation?
A Money Affirmation is a Personal Line in the Sand. It defines who you refuse to be with money and who you are becoming. These affirmations help you align your People decisions, your Process choices, and your Perspective about money — creating a healthier, more confident financial identity.
The purpose of this tool
This tool helps you draw your own lines in the sand. You will choose 3–5 money beliefs you refuse to carry any longer, then 3–5 affirmations for who you are becoming — and seal them with a declaration that you and your coach sign together.
There are 4 pages. Use the Next and Back buttons to move through them. On the selection pages, drag and drop each statement you choose into your list (on a phone or tablet, just tap a statement to add it).
