HOP Goal Setting Sheet

Habits · Outcomes · Performance

Great financial goals are built on three layers. Habits are the small repeatable actions you control every day. Outcomes are the results those habits produce over time. Performance is how consistently you show up and how you measure it. Work down the sheet with your coach — habits first.

H — Habits

The daily and weekly actions you fully control. Small, repeatable, and specific enough to tick off.

O — Outcomes

The results your habits create — the number, the milestone, the change you'll be able to point at.

P — Performance

How you'll measure consistency — the check-in, the score, the review that keeps you honest.

Habit examples
•Check my account balance every morning before coffee.
•Move $25 to savings every payday, automatically.
•Wait 24 hours before any purchase over my Press Pause Number.
•Pack lunch four weekdays out of five.
Outcome examples
•$1,000 starter emergency fund in place.
•Smallest credit card paid to zero.
•Rent paid on the 1st for six months straight.
•Two unused subscriptions cancelled.
Performance examples
•Weekly 15-minute money check-in every Sunday.
•Track habit streak on the calendar — aim for 4/5 days.
•Review spending buckets with my coach each month.
•Score myself 1–5 on consistency at every session.

TFS Principles

  1. Habits before outcomes. You control the action, not the result.
  2. Small and specific wins. A goal you can do on a bad day is a goal that sticks.
  3. Measure what matters. If it isn't tracked, it isn't real.
  4. Progress over perfection. Four out of five days is a success, not a failure.
  5. Say it out loud. Commitments shared with someone else get kept.

💬 Coach Tip

If a client jumps straight to an outcome ("I want to be debt free"), gently walk them back a step: "What would you have to do every week for that to happen?" The answer is the habit — write that down first. Outcomes without habits are wishes.
