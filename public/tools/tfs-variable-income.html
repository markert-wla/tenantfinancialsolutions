Variable / Gig Income Allocation & Stabilizer

A step-by-step interactive tool for tenants with irregular, commission, or hourly shifts to define their Baseline Survival Target, build a Low-Month Survival Plan, and assign every extra dollar in High Months using a clear priority ladder—ending with a “fixed salary” Income Stabilizer method.

Step 2 · Low-Month Survival Plan

When monthly earnings only cover or fall slightly below the Baseline Target.

Survival Mode
📊 Enter income and baseline to see if this is a Low Month or High Month.
Low-Month Rules
  • Fund Baseline First: Direct 100% of incoming funds toward Step 1 expenses.
  • Activate Emergency Buffer: If earnings fall short, draw only the exact difference from the Buffer Account.
  • Pause Non-Essentials: Freeze discretionary spending and extra debt payoff until income recovers.

Step 4 · Income Stabilizer (“Hill-Covering” Method)

Use a Buffer Account to pay yourself a fixed “salary” equal to your Baseline Target.

Fixed Paycheck
  • Separate Accounts: Maintain a separate Income Buffer Account alongside primary checking.
  • Deposit All Income: During high-earning months, deposit all revenue into the Buffer Account.
  • Pay a Fixed Salary: Transfer a consistent weekly or bi-weekly “salary” equal to the Baseline Target into checking.
